About Our Ministry

Empowering the Next Generation of Believers

The Seventh-day Adventist Youth Ministry is dedicated to leading young people into a personal, saving relationship with Jesus Christ and helping them develop into mature, active disciples.

Through interactive Bible studies, weekly Sabbath AY meetings, Pathfinder honor classes, outdoor camping, and community outreach, we build strong Christian leaders.

"Don't let anyone look down on you because you are young, but set an example for the believers in speech, in conduct, in love, in faith and in purity."

— 1 Timothy 4:12 (NIV)
